What Artham does

Four screens. One clear picture of your money.

Home

Every account, one balance.

Your total available balance sits at the top, with this month’s income and expenses right beneath it. Each bank gets its own card below.

  • Total across Federal Bank, ICICI Bank, State Bank of India and more
  • Income and expenses for the month at a glance
  • A card for each bank with its last four digits and balance
  • Balances that update as transactions change
  • A budget shortcut that opens the full Budgets screen

  • Add Transaction button always within reach

Insights

See where it goes.

Net savings and savings rate for the month, income against expenses over the last six months, and a breakdown that shows what takes the biggest share.

  • Net savings with a plain-language note on how you are doing
  • Six months of income and expenses, side by side
  • “Where your money goes” by category, with percentages
  • Budget performance, planned against actual, with month-end forecasts

Budgets

Stay inside the line.

Set one overall budget for the month, then give each category its own limit. Progress bars show what is used and what is left.

  • Total monthly budget with spent and remaining amounts
  • Per-category limits, each with “% used” and “left”
  • Step back through earlier months
  • Progress refreshes by itself when transactions change
  • Alerts when a budget reaches 80% and 100%
  • Opt-in rollover and twelve months of budget history

Carry it forward

Opt in to roll unused overall and category balances into the next month. Only positive balances carry over.

Set a limit once

Repeat overall or category limits every month, and pause or resume a template any time. Missing months fill in when you open the app. Existing budgets are never overwritten, and no transactions or balances are touched.

Plan against reality

A dashboard compares planned and actual spending for the overall budget and every category, with a six-month trend.

See the month-end

Forecasts use your average daily pace, flag budgets that are approaching or likely to exceed their limit, and suggest a daily spending guide. They appear after three days and refresh with Insights.

Alerts and history

Budget alerts at 80% and 100%, plus twelve months of budget history with a category breakdown.

Local-first

Your data stays on your phone.

What you see₹100.00Hover or tap to flip
What Artham stores10000Whole paise, never fractions of a rupee

Stored on the device

Artham keeps its data in an SQLite database on your phone, managed with Drift.

Exact to the paisa

Amounts are saved as whole paise, so floating-point rounding never creeps into your balances.

Upgrades keep your records

Database migrations are written to preserve existing data. The latest upgrades kept existing transactions and budgets intact.

Database encryption and other security improvements are planned.
